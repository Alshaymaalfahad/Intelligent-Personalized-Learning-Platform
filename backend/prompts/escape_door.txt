# The door

- `door_title`: short (e.g. «الباب الثاني: السجلات المكررة»).
- `scenario`: at most ~70 words. Continue the story; if the previous door's decision had a consequence (see previous levels: `consequence_of_decision`), this door must follow from it (e.g. rows were deleted → totals now don't match).
- `artifact_kind` / `artifact_lines`: evidence to inspect (a small table with " | " cells and a header line, a code/config/log excerpt, or a short text), 4–10 short lines; "none" with an empty list only if the door is a pure decision.
- `challenge_type`: "choice" (one correct option) or "multi_select" (2–3 correct options, e.g. «حدد الأعمدة التي فيها قيم ناقصة»). Vary across doors.
- `question` and 4–5 short `options` (one line each). Distractors are real mistakes people make on the job. The correct option(s) must not stand out by length.
- `option_consequences`: one short Arabic sentence per option describing what happens if chosen (a wrong choice has a realistic negative consequence; the right one moves the work forward).
- `justification_question` (e.g. «لماذا هذا هو القرار الصحيح؟») with 3 `justification_options`, exactly one correct; the wrong ones are the trainee's likely misconceptions.
- `clue_sheet`: the mentor's «ورقة دليل» — 2–3 sentences explaining ONLY the concept needed for this door, without giving the answer away directly.
- `hints`: 2 progressive hints (first vague, then more precise), never the answer.
- In the boss (final) door: a NEW situation that combines what the earlier doors exercised; still write hints and the clue sheet (they will not be shown).

# Always

- Set everything in realistic work at a company that delivers digital and technical solutions to clients; generic names only (no real companies or people). Do not literally repeat "at a digital solutions company".
- Keep texts short and quick to read. Clear Modern Standard Arabic for everything the trainee reads; technical terms in English where professionals use them; code in English.
- `mentor_message`: 1–2 warm Arabic sentences from the AI mentor opening this level; if there were previous levels, comment specifically on how the trainee did. Never discouraging: mistakes are part of learning.

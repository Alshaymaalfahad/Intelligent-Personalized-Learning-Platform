# Workplace realism (required)

Even for knowledge questions, anchor the content in the reality of a digital / technical solutions company: client projects, system design decisions, team practices, production systems, client requirements, security and quality standards. Scenario questions describe a short, concrete workplace situation. Lower-level questions (remembering / understanding) may be direct, but should still be about knowledge used on the job. Use generic names (no real companies or people), and do not literally repeat "at a digital solutions company" in every question.

# Keep it short (required)

The trainee should be able to read and answer in about one minute (open_ended: a 2–3 sentence answer).
- `question_text`: at most ~60 words. Each option: one short line (at most ~15 words). `code_snippet` rarely needed; at most 12 lines.

# Question types

- `true_false`: one clear statement; `options` must be exactly {{tf_options}}; `correct_option_index` 0 = true, 1 = false. No absolute words used as hints, no double negatives. Use {{tf_hint}} as the correct answer for this one.
- `multiple_choice`: a conceptual question with 4 options.
- `scenario`: a short workplace situation where the right choice depends on understanding a concept (4 options).
- `ordering`: the trainee orders 4–5 short items — the stages of a process, the layers of a model, or the steps of a method. Put them in `options` IN THE CORRECT ORDER (the server shuffles them); `correct_option_index` = -1. Exactly one sequence must be correct. Do not number the steps (no "1)", "2.", "Step 1:") — the page numbers them.
- `open_ended`: the trainee explains, compares or justifies in 2–3 sentences; include 2–4 explicit rubric criteria worth 1–2 points each (total 2–6).

# Quality rules

1. Measure exactly the given knowledge area at the target Bloom level.
2. Self-contained and unambiguous; exactly one option is defensibly correct.
3. Distractors reflect real misconceptions; all options have similar length and style; the correct option must NOT be the longest or most detailed.
4. For 4-option questions, place the correct option at index {{correct_index_hint}} (0-based).
5. Never use "all of the above", "none of the above", or combined options.
6. No trick questions, no pure trivia, no unnecessary cultural assumptions.
7. Put any code only in `code_snippet` (empty string if none).
8. `question_text` must not mention the area name, the Bloom level, the difficulty, or that this is a follow-up.

# Fields

- `question_type`: exactly the type given above.
- `learning_objective`: one specific, measurable objective at the target Bloom level.
- `correct_option_index`: 0-based index; -1 for ordering and open_ended.
- `scoring_rubric`: only for open_ended; empty list otherwise.
- `reference_answer`: the correct answer plus one sentence on why (ordering: the correct sequence); for open_ended, the expected answer elements.
- `rationale`: why the correct answer is correct and why the most tempting wrong answer is wrong (or what the open task reveals).

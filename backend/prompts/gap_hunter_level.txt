The trainee is shown a realistic work artifact (a table, code, config, log, report or email) containing hidden mistakes. They hunt each mistake by clicking its line, classify it, choose why it is wrong, and choose the fix. The game must close one specific gap that the assessment found.

# The artifact

- A realistic work product a first-year employee would review at such a company: a client data extract, an API response, a SQL query, a function, a deployment config, a log excerpt, a test plan, a requirements note, a status email… Pick what best exercises the competency above. Use generic names (no real companies or people).
- 8 to 14 lines. Keep every line short (at most ~120 characters).
  - `table`: the first line is the header; cells separated by " | ". Mistakes are never on the header line.
  - `code` / `config` / `log`: one code line per item, in English, in a language or tool common for this specialization.
  - `text`: one sentence or bullet per item, in Arabic (technical terms in English).
- Exactly {{error_count}} lines contain a mistake, at most one mistake per line, spread across the artifact (not all adjacent).
- EVERY OTHER LINE MUST BE COMPLETELY CORRECT. A careful expert must find nothing wrong outside the intended lines; otherwise the trainee is punished for correct clicks. Check this before answering.
- Each mistake must relate to the competency and be realistic (a mistake people really make), not a typo or a trick.
- If a mistake involves more than one line (e.g. a duplicated record: the original and the copy, or two lines that contradict each other), put the mistake on one line and list the other line(s) in `related_lines` — clicking any of them counts as finding it. Otherwise `related_lines` is an empty list.

# Categories, explanations, fixes, hints

- `categories`: 4–5 short Arabic category names for this artifact (e.g. "قيمة ناقصة", "تكرار", "صيغة خاطئة", "قيمة شاذة"). Every mistake's `category` must be exactly one of them; include 1–2 plausible categories that no mistake uses.
- For each mistake:
  - `explanation_options`: 3 short Arabic options for WHY it is wrong; exactly one correct; the others are plausible misconceptions (use the trainee's misconceptions from the evidence where possible).
  - `fix_options`: 3 short options for the fix; exactly one correct.
  - Options similar in length; the correct one must not stand out.
  - `hint_1`: vague — where to look (e.g. "راجع الأعمدة المتعلقة بالتواريخ"), without naming the line or the answer.
  - `hint_2`: more precise — what to pay attention to, still without giving the fix.

# Teaching when the trainee is wrong

`line_notes`: exactly one item per line (same count as `lines`). For every CORRECT line, one short Arabic sentence explaining why it is fine even if it looks suspicious — teach the distinction, e.g. «هذا مو خطأ: الرقم كبير لكنه صحيح لأنه موسم تخفيضات. الخطأ الحقيقي يكون لما الرقم مستحيل منطقيًا، مثل عمر عميل 250 سنة.». For the header and for mistake lines use an empty string.

# Mentor

`mentor_message`: 1–2 short, warm Arabic sentences from the AI mentor opening this level. If there were previous levels, comment briefly and specifically on how the trainee did (what they caught, what they missed, hints used) and what to watch for now. Never discouraging: mistakes are part of learning.

# Language

Arabic (clear Modern Standard Arabic) for everything the trainee reads, except code/config/log lines.
